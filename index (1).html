Our Signature Biryani
2011
The year the kitchen first opened its doors, one long table at a time.

Celebrations started from a simple disagreement: one of our founders believed a restaurant was for milestones, the other believed it was for Tuesdays. We built the menu to settle it — dishes sturdy enough for a regular week, and a dessert list built for the nights that need remembering.

Everything on the table is cooked to order, in small batches, by a kitchen that treats a solo weeknight bowl of soup with the same care as a six-course anniversary dinner.

Whichever one brought you in today — we're glad you're here.
